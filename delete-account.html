Delete your account and data

Nilphamari App · Developer: Nazmul Huda Shohag · Last updated: 26 October 2026

Option 1: Delete it yourself in the app (fastest)

  1. Open the Nilphamari app and sign in with the Google account you used.
  2. Go to the Profile tab in the bottom bar.
  3. Scroll to the bottom, tap Delete account and confirm.

This cannot be undone.

Option 2: Request by email

If you cannot open the app or have uninstalled it, email nhshohagnh7704@gmail.com with the subject Delete my Nilphamari account. Please send it from the Google email address you signed in with so we can verify it is you. We will delete your data and confirm within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile: name, email, gender, date of birth, blood group, education, marital status, area, mobile number.
  • Points, points history, daily activity, referral code and referral records, your "My posts" history.
  • All approved listings under your account (buy/sell, rent, tutor wanted), including phone numbers.
  • All your pending submissions awaiting review.

What may remain

  • Directory entries: approved entries in directories (doctors, hospitals, blood donors, restaurants, banks, schools, etc.) are not linked to your account and stay after you delete it. If you submitted one and want it removed, email us and we will remove it within 30 days.
  • Backups: deleted data may remain in the server's security backups for a short time (usually a few weeks) before it is erased automatically.

Want to delete only some data?

To clear some profile details without deleting your account, open Profile → Personal info, empty the fields and save. To remove one listing, open Profile → My posts and tap "Remove".
